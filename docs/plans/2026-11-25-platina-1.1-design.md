# Platina 1.1: PS4, friends, instant trophies

2026-11-25. The owner's decisions in the conversation of this date; what is marked "builder's
call" was decided while writing this, not by the owner.

## Why

Version 1.0 goes to App Store review as it is. 1.1 closes what docs/roadmap.md left for later,
because the owner wants the app complete: the PS4 games in the library, friends to compare with,
and trophies that arrive as notifications even when the app was not opened at all.

## Goals

1. **PS4.** PS4 games are in the library, the game screen, sessions, guides and walkthroughs like
   PS5 games.
2. **Friends.** See who is ahead in the games you share with each PSN friend: progress only
   (percent, trophies earned, platinum), no trophy-by-trophy comparison.
3. **Instant trophies.** A notification for every trophy within about 1–3 minutes of earning it,
   with the app closed; several trophies of one check in one notification; the platinum alone, as
   a celebration.

## Non-goals

- Trophy-by-trophy comparison with a friend; "a friend overtook you" notifications; adding or
  messaging friends.
- Updating the session's Live Activity from the server (builder's call: a Live Activity push must
  carry the whole content state, which only the app knows; the app updates it when opened, as now).
- PS3 and Vita.

## Anti-goals

- Spending Sony's unpublished request limits: no polling of anyone who is not playing, no
  refreshing friends more than twice a day unasked.
- The server knowing more than it needs: no passwords, no NPSSO, no trophy history beyond the last
  check, nothing of friends.
- Breaking 1.0's promise for people who don't turn the notifications on: their tokens stay only
  on the phone.

## Constraints

- PSN's API is unofficial; every request lives in PSNKit, tested on JSON fixtures.
- App Store: storing a PSN grant on the server needs an opt-in, the privacy policy and App Privacy
  updated (the owner updates App Privacy; the policy page is ours).
- The server is the existing Cloudflare Worker (`server/assistant`), App Attest on every write.
- APNs needs the owner's `.p8` key: secrets `APNS_KEY_P8`, `APNS_KEY_ID` (team `SY2L2VMQP7`,
  topic `dakacom.Platina`).

## Approaches (instant trophies)

1. **A Durable Object per subscriber with its own alarm** (chosen). It keeps the encrypted grant,
   the device's push token and the last check; it wakes itself: every 5 minutes it asks PSN whether
   the person is playing, while they play every minute it checks the trophy list, and it sends the
   push itself. Same Worker, same deploy; one person never slows another.
2. A cron every minute walking all subscribers: simpler, but one run for everyone hits CPU limits
   and KV's eventual consistency can send a notification twice.
3. A separate polling server (VPS): most flexible, but a new service and bill for the owner.

The owner picked 1 (2026-11-25).

## Design

### PS4

- `LibrarySync` stops filtering `isPS5`: trophy lists of PS4 games (`npServiceName` `trophy`) and
  their play time (`gamelist/v2` already asks `ps4_game`) come in.
- `GameRecord` keeps the platform (`platform`: `PS5`, `PS4`, from `trophyTitlePlatform`); the
  library and game rows show "PS4" in their meta line; the library's filters gain a platform chip
  row only when the person has both (builder's call).
- Game Help is a PS5 feature: the trophy screen and Plati's `get_game_help` skip it for PS4.
- Art: PS4 games have no `HERO_CHARACTER`; the hero falls back to the cover as for PS5 games
  without one.
- The App Store texts say "PS5 and PS4" (`appstore/metadata.json`).

### Friends

- PSNKit: `friends()` (`userProfile/v1/internal/users/me/friends`, account ids), `profiles(of:)`
  (online id, avatar; batched), `trophyTitles(of accountId:)` (the friend's lists, same model as
  mine); a friend who hides trophies answers 403 → `.hidden`.
- Store (cache, not iCloud): `FriendRecord` (account id, online id, avatar URL, hidden, updated)
  and `FriendTitleRecord` (friend id, npCommunicationId, progress, earned counts, platinum, last
  updated). `FriendsSync` refreshes when the friends screen or a game's friends section opens and
  the data is older than 12 hours, or on pull to refresh; requests one by one.
- Profile → "Friends": a card list of friends (avatar, name, "ahead in 7 of 12 shared games" or
  "hides trophies"). A friend's screen: the summary, then the shared games with both progresses
  side by side (who is ahead marked), sorted by the gap.
- The game screen: a "Friends" section (a chip on a phone, a card beside on wide screens): the
  friends who have this game and you, ranked by progress, you in your place.
- Strings in both languages; skeletons and empty states (no friends, nobody shares the game).

### Instant trophies

**Turning it on** (Settings → "Trophy notifications", and the onboarding's notifications page):
the app asks for notification permission, registers for remote notifications, then opens Sony's
sign-in once more: from its NPSSO it asks Sony for a second authorization code (`authorize`, as
the sign-in does) and sends only that code to the server, which exchanges it for its own token pair
(so the phone's and the server's refresh tokens never step on each other). Off, sign-out, or
"Remove from the server" → `DELETE`; the server forgets everything.

**Server** (`server/assistant`, builder's call on names):

- `POST /v1/alerts` (App Attest): `{ code, pushToken, environment: sandbox|production, language }`
  → the Worker exchanges the code at `ca.account.sony.com` (the PlayStation App client, as
  PSNKit), reads the account id from the access token, and hands everything to the Durable Object
  `TrophyWatcher` named by the App Attest key id. `PUT /v1/alerts/token` updates the push token;
  `DELETE /v1/alerts` stops and erases.
- `TrophyWatcher` (SQLite-backed Durable Object): stores the refresh token encrypted (AES-GCM, key
  in the secret `TOKEN_KEY`), the access token while it lasts, the push token and environment, the
  language, and the last trophy check (per list: earned counts and `lastUpdatedDateTime`).
  - Alarm, idle: every 5 minutes `basicPresences` of the account; playing → active. Presence hidden
    → a trophy-list check every 10 minutes instead.
  - Alarm, active: every 60 seconds the first page of `trophyTitles` (sorted by update); a list
    that changed → its earned trophies and definitions → the new ones → push. Two quiet checks after
    the person stops playing → idle.
  - First check after subscribing only records the state (no flood of old trophies).
  - Refresh token refused or expired → one push "Sign in again to keep trophy notifications" and
    the watcher stops.
- APNs: token-based (`APNS_KEY_P8`, `APNS_KEY_ID`, ES256 JWT reused for 50 minutes), HTTP/2 by
  `fetch`, `api.push.apple.com` or `api.sandbox.push.apple.com` by the app's environment; 410 →
  the watcher stops. Payload: alert title and body in the person's language ("🏆 Gold · Night
  Rider — Emberfall", or "3 trophies in Emberfall"), `thread-id` per game, `gameId` and trophy ids
  to open the game; the platinum is its own notification with its own sound.
- The deploy workflow uploads `APNS_KEY_P8`, `APNS_KEY_ID` and creates `TOKEN_KEY` once if missing.

**App:** with the notifications on, the background refresh no longer posts its own local trophy
notifications (no duplicates); a tap on a notification opens the game.

**Privacy:** the policy page says what the server keeps and why, only for those who turn it on;
App Privacy gains "User ID (PSN account id) — linked, App Functionality" (the owner updates it in
App Store Connect when 1.1 ships); the review notes explain the opt-in.
